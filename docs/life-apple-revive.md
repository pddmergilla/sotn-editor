`node tools/items/build-life-apple-revive-patch.js <output-directory>` prepares an exact-image forward/reversal PPF pair for the ASS 2.0 BIN that already has the Life Apple auto-revive (v1, 2026-10-12). The original BIN is not changed. `--apply` requires the user's explicit approval of that prepared pair and checks its full source hash.

**Charges.** You get min(Life Apples held, 5) charges. Each automatic revive uses one; apples are not consumed. A save point, loading a save, or `InitStatsAndGear` recharges them all.

**HUD.** v1 put the raw GPU CLUT `0x3F8E` in `prim->clut`. `RenderPrimitives` uses that field as an index into `g_ClutIds`, so the icons were drawn with an unrelated palette, and at 16x16 they were large. v2 draws up to five 8x8 copies of the Life Apple icon (154, palette 149) at x 36 + 9n, y 34, just under the ASS HUD box (ASS moved the HUD up 14 px). Gold apples are charges left and grey ones are used; apples beyond min(held, 5) are hidden. The five GT4 prims are allocated at the end of `DrawHud`, like the HUD's own, and redrawn at the end of `DrawHudSubweapon`. The texture and gold CLUT use equip-icon slot 30 (`LoadEquipIcon`, CLUT index `0x1EE`). The grey CLUT, built from palette 149 at build time, lives in slot 29's palette (index `0x1ED`). It is marked with `0xFFFE` in the slot's palette cache, so it is rewritten if anything else loads into slot 29.

**Revive.** Both copies of `PlayerStepKill`'s `unk28 == 0xFFF && step_s` test call `check`. It starts a revive when step_s > 0, unk28 is free, no revive is running and a charge is left. `PlayerStepUnk17` then calls `tick` every frame:

1. 30 frames after death, one full-screen G4 appears. It is additive and dithered, with priority 0x1C0, so it sits under the HUD.
2. Its brightness rises 4 per frame to 128 over 32 frames.
3. At the peak, HP and MP refill and entities 8-63 are destroyed, as the Faerie's `UpdateServantUseLifeApple` does.
4. The glow fades 2 per frame. At frame 96 the prim is freed and unk28 is released, about 2.1 s after death.
5. Vanilla `PlayerStepUnk17` then stands Alucard up with its white blink.

The kill and tick hooks are `jal`s that return to the hook's own fall-through path, or the original branch target (+0x34 / +0x50). v1's TT_002 change, which stops the Faerie using Life Apples herself, is kept.

**Space.** The helper fills DRA `0x2E624-0x2EAA3` exactly: v1's space in icons 294-301 plus blank icon 302. Icon 303 belongs to another mod. RAM variables are at `0x800CDCA4-0x800CDCAB` in blank icon 275. Do not assign icons 294-302 to items. There is no spare room for additions; a larger change needs new space.
