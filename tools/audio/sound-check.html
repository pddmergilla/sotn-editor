SotN Sound Browser

Inspect, play and download the sound effects and samples of Castlevania: Symphony of the Night, read straight from your BIN.

Import your SotN BIN (or drop it on this page). It is read inside this page: it is never uploaded or modified. Sample previews include paired tones and sound-script timing, with approximate pitch and volume; previews stop after eight seconds. Reverb, envelopes, looping and channel stealing are not emulated. Raw samples play once at the chosen sample rate. Exports are the samples’ original PlayStation ADPCM data (.VAG) or whole banks (.VH + .VB); WAV files are decoded previews.

Import a SotN BIN to start.

Pick a sound

Pick a row to see its RAM address, BIN offset and source references.

Stage and boss sound names depend on the loaded bank. A playable slot alone does not confirm its name in that bank.

Source matches are references, not a complete runtime trace. Some code belongs to other builds. Unknown and unused slots stay labeled as such.

Streamed XA dialogue, movies and music are a separate audio system and are not covered here.
